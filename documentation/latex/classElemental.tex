\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{classElemental}{Elemental} enemy with damage type interactions. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classEnemy}{Enemy}. Has FIRE or ICE affinity and reacts differently to elemental attacks. Can heal from same element or take double damage from opposite.
